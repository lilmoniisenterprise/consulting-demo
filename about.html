A small firm, by design.

Aldermoor (fictional) was founded on a simple idea: senior advice should be delivered by senior people, priced clearly and written so anyone can use it.

01 Our story

From operators, for operators.

Our founding partner spent fifteen years running a family-owned distribution business before turning to advisory work. She had hired consultants and learned what she valued: people who listened before recommending, who were honest about trade-offs and who left her team stronger.

Aldermoor was built around those values. We stay deliberately small so every client works directly with a partner. We price engagements up front, because uncertainty about fees is the last thing a leadership team needs. And we write plainly, because a plan that cannot be understood will not be followed.

Today the firm advises owner-led and mid-market businesses across North America on strategy, finance, operations, marketing and growth.

02 Principles

Four commitments we make to every client.

Principle 01

Listen first

We do not arrive with answers. Every engagement starts with interviews and data, so recommendations reflect your business rather than a template.

Principle 02

Say it plainly

Findings are written in clear language with evidence attached. If something is uncomfortable, we say so early and privately.

Principle 03

Price it up front

Scope and fees are agreed in writing before work begins. Changes are discussed, never surprised on an invoice.

Principle 04

Leave capability behind

We measure success by what your team can do after we leave: the models, routines and confidence they keep.

04 How we work

Hybrid by default, in the room when it counts.

Interviews and analysis are mostly remote, which keeps fees down. Workshops, offsites and operational pilots happen on site, because some work needs people in the same room.

Every engagement follows the same rhythm: a written scope, a weekly update, a mid-point review and a closing session where we hand over everything we built.

05 FAQ

Common questions.

Can't find what you need? Send us a note.

What size of business do you work with?

Mostly owner-led and mid-market firms with roughly 15 to 500 employees, across professional services, distribution, construction and light manufacturing.

How are engagements priced?

Most work is fixed-fee for a defined scope, agreed in writing before we start. Retained advisory is a monthly fee. We do not bill by the hour for project work.

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. The 30-minute introductory call is complimentary and carries no obligation.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Many engagements are hybrid: remote interviews and analysis, with on-site workshops at key points.

How do you handle confidential information?

We sign a mutual confidentiality agreement before receiving sensitive material and limit access to the engagement team.
