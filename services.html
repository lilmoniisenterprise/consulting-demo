— FAQ

Questions about engagements.

Request a consultation
What size of business do you work with?

Mostly owner-led and mid-market firms with roughly 15 to 500 employees, across professional services, distribution, construction and light manufacturing.

How are engagements priced?

Most work is fixed-fee for a defined scope, agreed in writing before we start. Retained advisory is a monthly fee. We do not bill by the hour for project work.

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. The 30-minute introductory call is complimentary and carries no obligation.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Many engagements are hybrid: remote interviews and analysis, with on-site workshops at key points.

How do you handle confidential information?

We sign a mutual confidentiality agreement before receiving sensitive material and limit access to the engagement team.
