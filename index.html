01 The practice

Independent advice, written plainly and owned by you.

Who we serveOwner-led and mid-market firms, typically 15–500 people, in professional services, distribution, construction and light manufacturing.

Most firms we meet are not short of ideas. They are short of time, distance and a shared view of what matters most. Our work starts by closing that gap: listening across the business, testing what we hear against the numbers, and saying clearly what we find.

From there we help you choose a small number of priorities and make them happen. Every engagement is led by a partner, scoped in writing and priced up front. When we leave, the plan, the tools and the capability stay with your team.
